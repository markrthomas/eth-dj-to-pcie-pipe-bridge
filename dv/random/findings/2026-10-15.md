## Clusters (Verilator, `make rr`)

Binaries: `B=dv/vlt/obj_dir` (vlt), `obj_dir_fc` (vlt-fc), `obj_dir_nodpr` (vlt-nodpr). Run from an empty dir that has `logs/`.

### A. `ERROR [corners]: frame aborted` (+ `frame length`, `frame payload` in every later scenario)
- Count: 59+61+54+42+47 = 263 (vlt) and 22+12+19+18+17 = 88 (vlt-nodpr) over 5 batches; first seen batch1 `c0`/`c3`.
- Minimal repro (no rand args, any seed; deterministic, reran twice in the sweep + run.log):
  `B/Veth_dj_pipe7_bridge +seed=1 +sink_pct=30` — also fails at sink_pct 40 (14 errors) and below; passes at >=50 on `vlt` and `vlt-nodpr`.
  Sweep (seed 1, mac_gap 10): sink_pct 90/70/50 PASS; 40/30/20/10/5 FAIL on both `vlt` and `vlt-nodpr`; `vlt-fc` PASSES at every sink_pct 5..90.
- Class: **harness stimulus envelope / TB expectation** (not DUT). Evidence: only the non-flow-control builds fail, only when the Ethernet sink
  drains slower than ~45 % of cycles (Rx path oversubscribed), and the FC build is lossless for the same stimulus. docs/OPEN_DECISIONS.md D9
  specifies exactly this: without credits an Rx overload drops flits and aborts the damaged frame (RXCNT0/RXCNT1, `eth_rx_tuser[0]`).
  `bridge_bfm.h:301` treats any abort in the fixed scenarios as an error, so the runner's `sink_pct` range (100/90/50/20/5, `rr.py:43`) leaves the
  contract of the non-FC DUT. `rand` scenario itself did not fail in these runs.
- Suspected files: `dv/random/rr.py` (KNOBS), `dv/common/cpp/bridge_bfm.h` (:301, sink_ready); not rtl/.
- Suggested follow-up (not done): constrain `sink_pct >= 50` for non-FC configs (or make the checker accept flagged aborts when sink < threshold)
  — but only the owner/debugger should choose; do not weaken the check.

### B. `ERROR [rxovf]: rxovf: credit flow control lost frames` (vlt-fc)
- Count: 13+16+12+16+9 = 66 (every vlt-fc job with mac_gap=90 and rxovf reached).
- Minimal repro: `obj_dir_fc/Veth_dj_pipe7_bridge +seed=1 +mac_gap=90` (sink_pct irrelevant; seed irrelevant). Deterministic (reran twice).
  Sweep (fc, seed 1): mac_gap 0..85 PASS; 90/93/95/97/98/99 FAIL with frames received 58/53/45/31/18/9 of 60 — monotonically fewer as mac_gap rises.
- Class: **TB / scenario timing** (not DUT). Evidence: delivered-frame count falls smoothly with MAC idle %, no frame is flagged aborted (that would raise
  "frame aborted although credit flow control is on") and no unflagged mismatch is reported: the frames simply had not arrived when the rxovf scenario's
  fixed idle windows (`OP_IDLE 60000` / `20000`, `bridge_bfm.h:444-449`) expired. The scenario check at `bridge_bfm.h:487`
  (`res_.frames != 60`) assumes the source finishes inside the window.
- Suspected files: `dv/common/cpp/bridge_bfm.h` (scenario 6 timing, :444-449, :487); `dv/random/rr.py` (mac_gap=90 allowed with fixed windows).

### C. `ERROR [<scenario>]: scenario op timeout` (vlt, vlt-nodpr; vlt-fc once with maxlen 9000)
- Count: 3+5+4+2+5 = 19 (vlt), 1+1+1+... = ~6 (vlt-nodpr) plus vlt-fc `[rand]` x2 (batch3 `c2`: seeds 3016, and one more, `mac_gap 0/5 sink_pct 5 rand_maxlen 9000 rand_frames 96`).
- Repro: `obj_dir/Veth_dj_pipe7_bridge +seed=1222 +mac_gap=60 +sink_pct=5` (batch1 c1; no rand args). Deterministic. At sink_pct 8/10/15 the same seed gives class A instead.
- Class (non-FC): **same root as A**, TB/harness. Evidence: run.log shows `corners frames=8` of 11 while all 58 flits were sent; frames whose `sof` flit
  was dropped are silently orphaned (D9: orphan continuation flits dropped, counted in RXCNT1 — no abort emitted), so the sink's frame count never reaches
  `OP_WAIT_RX n` and the 400000-cycle op budget (`bridge_bfm.h:476`) expires.
  Observation for the owner (possible DUT-side issue, *not* classified as a bug): a whole frame can vanish with no `tuser[0]` indication when its first flit
  is dropped on overflow, which is weaker than D9's "never deliver a silently corrupted frame" text suggests; verify intent.
- Class (vlt-fc `[rand]`, sink_pct 5 + rand_frames 96 + rand_maxlen 9000): **harness timeout budget** — ~430 kB at 5 % sink needs >400k cycles; lossless build, just slow.
  Repro: batch3 `j00016` (`obj_dir_fc/... +seed=3016 +mac_gap=0 +sink_pct=5 +rand_seed=1180259679 +rand_frames=96 +rand_maxlen=9000`).
- Suspected files: `bridge_bfm.h` (:476 op budget, scenario WAIT_RX), `rr.py` (rand_frames x rand_maxlen x sink_pct combinations).

Residual check: after removing A/B/C-explained jobs (non-FC & sink<=40, FC & mac_gap>=90 & rxovf, sink<=5 timeouts) **0 of 443 failures remain** unexplained.
Caveat: a job that fails for an envelope reason can mask a second, independent failure behind it; a rerun with the knobs pinned inside the envelope
(non-FC sink_pct>=50, mac_gap<=70) was not done as a separate batch — see "not run".

### D. `iv-loop-x4`: `FAIL: frame N length N, expected N` — 16 + 16 = 32 failures
- Minimal repro: `vvp dv/iverilog/sim_build_fcrr_x4/loop.vvp +seed=1` (`FAIL: frame 11 length 4321, expected 9000`); `+seed=3` also fails, `+seed=2` passes; no `+seed` (directed) PASSES.
  Batch repro `+seed=293688134` (frame 10 length 1200, expected 1500) reran twice, identical.
- Class: **TB-envelope / (likely) same non-FC overload as A**, not yet proven. Evidence: truncated lengths are all `k*240+1` or multiples of the 240 B flit payload
  (1200, 481, 720, 961, 240) = frames cut at a flit boundary; scen-x4 shows `err 1` (aborted); failure rate grows with lower `rdy_min`
  (iv-loop-x4: 5/5 at rdy_min 50-60, 3/3 at 61-70, 5/8 at 71-80) and no x1 job fails. x4 moves 4x the bits per flit slot, so the Eth sink (same 256 bit/ eth_clk) is overrun at
  a sink readiness that x1 survives. Unproven: no overload counter (RXCNT0) was read — decisive check = print `rx_dropped_flits` at end of tb_loop.
  Non-trivial: loop fails even at rdy_min 91-100 (1/3), where the seeded sink should be nearly always ready — if it persists with `rr_rdy` pinned at 100 it is a **DUT** x4 bug.
- Suspected files: `dv/iverilog/tb_loop.sv`, `rr_seed.svh` (sink floor 50 % is below the x4 sustainable rate), `rtl/rx_ingress.sv` / `rx_deframer.sv` / `rx_cdc.sv` (drop + abort at overload).

### F. `iv-link-x4`: `FAIL: P4 all 5 kills were applied` — 2 failures (batch6 i00048 seed 909855594, i00131 seed 423184446)
- Repro: `vvp dv/iverilog/sim_build_fcrr_x4_fc/link.vvp +seed=909855594` (kills=4, aborted=2, skipped=2). Deterministic x2. Flow control is ON here, so not an overload class.
- Class: **TB** (stimulus timing sized for x1). `tb_link.sv:293-309`: the 5 flit kills are issued at fixed 600+300k pclk offsets; each waits up to 20000 cycles for a flit to kill.
  At x4 the 110-170 payload burst can finish before the 5th kill, so no flit is left to kill and `kills_done==5` fails. Other P4 checks (`corrupt==0`, `skipped+errf<=kills`) passed in the same runs. Not yet proven by tracing the kill timestamps.
- Suspected files: `dv/iverilog/tb_link.sv` (:293-309).
